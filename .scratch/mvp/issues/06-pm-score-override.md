# 06: PM score override

**What to build:** A PM can override a Problem's Priority score with a required note, and clear the override later. Internal stakeholders see the override, its note, and the computed score side by side, and the override is used for sorting.

**Blocked by:** 05

**Status:** done

- [x] Clearing the override restores the computed score
- [x] The Problem page and internal home list show the override clearly, with the computed score still visible
- [x] Support agents and Customers can't override; Customers never see scores
